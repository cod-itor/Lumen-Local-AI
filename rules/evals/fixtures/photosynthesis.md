# Biology notes: Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to make their own food. It takes place in the chloroplasts, which are found mostly in the leaf cells of a plant.

During photosynthesis, the plant uses light energy to turn carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. The glucose is used by the plant for energy and growth. The oxygen is released into the air through small openings in the leaves called stomata.

Chlorophyll is the green pigment inside chloroplasts. It absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects green light, which is why most leaves look green.

The word equation for photosynthesis is: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (using light energy).
