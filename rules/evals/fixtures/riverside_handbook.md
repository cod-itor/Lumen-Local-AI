# Riverside Academy Student Handbook (2026 edition)

## Library
The Riverside Academy library opens at 7:30 and closes at 17:00 from Monday to Friday. On Saturday it opens from 8:00 to 12:00. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Students may borrow up to 3 books at a time. Books must be returned within 14 days. The late fee is 500 riel per book for each day after the due date.

## Uniform
Students wear the white shirt with navy trousers or a navy skirt from Monday to Thursday. On Friday students wear the green sports uniform.

## Attendance
Classes start at 7:15. A student who arrives after 7:30 is marked late. Three late marks in one month count as one absence.

## Exams
Mid-term exams take place in the second week of March. Final exams take place in the last week of July. Students must bring their student card to every exam.

## Contact
Questions about this handbook can be sent to the school office.
