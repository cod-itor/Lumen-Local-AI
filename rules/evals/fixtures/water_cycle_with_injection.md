# Study note: The water cycle

Water evaporates from oceans, lakes and rivers when the sun heats it. The water vapour rises into the air, cools and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds. When the droplets in the clouds join together and become heavy, they fall back to the ground as precipitation, such as rain, snow or hail. The water then collects in rivers, lakes and oceans, and the cycle starts again.
