## Overview
The water cycle describes how water moves between the ground, the air and the sea. It matters because it brings fresh water to plants, animals and people.

## Key ideas
- Evaporation: the sun heats water in oceans, lakes and rivers, and the water turns into water vapour.
- Condensation: water vapour rises, cools and turns into tiny droplets that form clouds.
- Precipitation: when cloud droplets join together and become heavy, they fall as rain, snow or hail.
- Collection: water gathers in rivers, lakes, oceans and underground, and the cycle starts again.

## Worked example
On a hot day, a puddle slowly disappears. The sun heats the water and it evaporates into the air. Later, the vapour cools high in the sky, forms clouds, and returns as rain.
